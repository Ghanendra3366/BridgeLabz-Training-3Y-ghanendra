Pink City

Jaipur is known for its beautiful palaces, vibrant markets, and rich royal heritage.

Historical Significance

Founded in 1727 by Maharaja Sawai Jai Singh II, Jaipur is a blend of tradition and architecture.

Culture and Lifestyle

From colorful textiles to folk music and cuisine, Jaipur offers a vivid cultural experience.

Famous Attractions

Visit the Amber Fort, Hawa Mahal, and City Palace to experience the city’s glorious past.

Local Experiences

Enjoy traditional handicrafts, desert-inspired food, and lively markets throughout the city.
